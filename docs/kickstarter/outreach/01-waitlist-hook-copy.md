## Version A: The Nod ⭐ recommended for the hero

**Headline:** Anything big waits for your nod.

**Subhead:** Piblo is a pocket companion with real keys. It reminds you what matters, saves what you say, and holds anything big (a payment, a message to a customer, a deleted file) until you press yes. No apps, no feeds, no subscription.

**Button:** Join the launch list

**Microcopy:** Free. No card. We'll email you when the Kickstarter opens. Works with a Windows PC on your Wi-Fi.

**Why:** Ties the brand line *Ask. Listen. Nod.* to one clear benefit anyone understands (control), without sounding like an AI gadget.

## Version B: Phone-free

**Headline:** Put your phone in a drawer. Piblo lets through only what matters.

**Subhead:** Reminders with one-press yes, no or later. Messages only from the people you choose. Voice notes saved to your own PC. A small screen with real keys, built to give your attention back.

**Button:** Join the launch list

**Microcopy:** Same as A.

**Why:** Strongest for the focus/ADHD and digital-minimalism audience; contrasts directly with phones and watches.

## Version C: Follow me (best for video and social)

**Headline:** "When the pasta's done, carry on my show on the TV."

**Subhead:** Piblo knows what you were watching on your laptop and moves it to the TV at the right moment, from the same second. Alexa can't, because it doesn't know what you were doing. Piblo's hub runs on your own PC, so it does.

**Button:** Join the launch list

**Why:** A concrete, filmable moment. Use as a second section or social hook, not the main hero (it's one feature, not the whole product).
