## Version B — Voice-note-first (Plaud/Friend contrast)

**Headline:** Hold a key. Talk. It's saved on your PC — not in our cloud.

**Subhead:** Voice notes and quick questions, answered by the AI on your Windows PC. Press Y / N / Later for reminders and agent approvals. No subscription, tips only if you ever want one.

**Button:** Save my spot — free, no payment

**Microcopy:** Free. No card today. Unsubscribe any time with one click. You need a Windows PC + Wi-Fi to use Piblo.

**Why:** Speaks directly to the two biggest wearable complaints (lost recordings / flaky sync; surprise subscription) with the on-device-first fix. Strong for the privacy/skeptic reader.

**Watch-outs:**
- "Not in our cloud" is only true if transcription uses a local model or the user's own key — keep the qualifier in FAQ for the cloud-model option.
- Do not claim "never lost" until local-first save + sync tick is shipped.
