# Piblo Kickstarter profit model: summary

Generated by `piblo_model.py` from `assumptions.csv`. All figures GBP, **net profit before business tax**, Maz's own time unpaid and not costed. **Nothing here is a supplier quote.** Every input is tagged QUOTED / SOURCED-LIST-PRICE / ASSUMPTION in `assumptions.csv`; at the time of writing no input is QUOTED and the Cardputer, Kickstarter fees, BackerKit fees and VAT threshold are the only list-price sourced inputs. Treat every output as a decision aid for what to quote next, not a forecast.

Definitions: *nominal units* = backers at the baseline prices (GBP 103 / 194) under expected demand; the demand level, price elasticity, early bird and shipping strategy then move actual backers. *Expected* = probability-weighted over conservative/expected/optimistic demand (35%/45%/20%, subjective). *Downside* = conservative demand, costs +15%, defects 7.5%, refunds +3pp, one GBP 5,000 unexpected expense. *Viable* = expected net > 0, goal met in the expected case, and downside loss no worse than GBP 15,000. All-or-nothing: if pledges miss the goal, the campaign pays nothing and only the pre-launch spend (prototypes, samples, video, legal) plus ads is lost; compliance and tooling are modelled as paid after funding.

Cash Maz must front **before** the Kickstarter payout (about 14 days after close): pre-launch spend plus ads plus supplier deposits (deposit 50% of goods + CNC setup) = about GBP 24,937 at 500 nominal and GBP 35,919 at 1,000. If M5Stack is full pre-pay (`dep_pct` = 1.0) it is higher. Pre-launch spend lost if the campaign misses its goal: GBP 10,300 plus ads.

Note the goal is a funding threshold, not a profit target: reaching it only covers a first batch and the one-offs. Profit needs several times the goal in backers (section 1).

## 9. How to read this honestly

- The model is internally consistent (checked by `test_model.py`: cash timeline sums to net profit; unit contributions reconcile to totals) but every cost is a list price or an assumption.
- Price elasticity (`elast_o`, `elast_f`) and demand scale are untested guesses. With the assumed elasticities, higher prices win because unit margin rises faster than demand falls; at the elasticity high case that reverses. Run a landing-page price test before trusting any price conclusion.
- Below roughly 1,000 backers fixed costs dominate; the project is a bet on volume. Staging spend (video, samples first; compliance after funding) caps what a miss costs.
- Excluded: corporation/income tax, Maz's time, FX moves on USD supplier prices, US tariffs on inbound parcels to backers, EU VAT/IOSS handling, chargebacks, rejected-campaign relaunch, retail/store sales after the campaign.
