# Nod: Kickstarter launch checklist (creator steps)

Kickstarter's process and requirements change. Check the current Creator Handbook and rules at each step. Items marked OWNER must be done personally: they involve identity, money or legal commitments that cannot be delegated to a draft or an assistant.

## Before creating the project
- [ ] OWNER: confirm every gate in `GATES.md` is MET.
- [ ] OWNER: read Kickstarter's rules and hardware/prototype requirements (real prototypes, no simulated functionality).
- [ ] OWNER: decide whether you launch as an individual or a registered business; check with an accountant (VAT, tax, liability).
- [ ] OWNER: confirm VAT registration position.

## Account and verification
- [ ] OWNER: create or confirm your Kickstarter account with your real name and email.
- [ ] OWNER: complete identity verification and add your bank account and payment details through Kickstarter. These must be yours.
- [ ] OWNER: confirm your country and campaign currency.

## Build the draft
- [ ] Start a project, choose the technology/hardware category, and enter the title, subtitle and blurb from `CAMPAIGN.md`.
- [ ] OWNER: set the funding goal from `ECONOMICS.md` and the campaign length.
- [ ] Paste the story sections. Replace every `[TBD]` and bracketed placeholder. Remove any claim you cannot demonstrate.
- [ ] Add video and photos: real device footage only, including the uncut demo.
- [ ] Add rewards from `REWARDS.md` with real prices, limits, shipping and estimated delivery.
- [ ] Rewrite the risks and challenges section in your own words.
- [ ] OWNER: add your creator bio and links.

## Prelaunch page
- [ ] OWNER: create the prelaunch ("notify me on launch") page if available to your account. Do not invent a follower count.
- [ ] Replace the website's mailto sign-up with a real double opt-in list before promoting, and state what data you hold.
- [ ] Share honest build updates (progress, failures, uncut clips) in maker and developer communities, following each community's self-promotion rules.

## Review and launch
- [ ] OWNER: submit the project for Kickstarter review. Reviewers may ask for changes, such as more proof of a working prototype. Allow time.
- [ ] Answer review feedback truthfully; do not stage footage.
- [ ] OWNER: choose the launch date and time. Announce no date or countdown until confirmed.
- [ ] OWNER: press Launch yourself. From here you are accepting pledges.

## After launch
- [ ] Post regular updates and answer backer messages promptly.
- [ ] Track real costs against the model, including fees, exchange rates and funds received.
- [ ] Tell backers early about any delay.
- [ ] Plan fulfilment: surveys, addresses, battery-compliant shipping, VAT and customs, returns.

## What an assistant can and cannot do
An assistant can draft text, build spreadsheets and prepare checklists. It cannot verify your identity, enter bank details, accept Kickstarter's terms for you, submit for review or launch. Do those yourself.
