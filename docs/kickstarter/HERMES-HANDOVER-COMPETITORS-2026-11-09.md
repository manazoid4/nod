## 3. Complaints people have, and the Piblo answer (use in posts and FAQ)
Only claim what is true today; mark the rest "in development".
1. **Late shipping, silent creators** (Kode Dot, 61% of KS projects ship late) → promise a fixed fortnightly update with "done / at risk / late" milestones.
2. **Device dies when the company does** (Humane, Limitless) → "Works without us": Core runs on your PC, open source, no account.
3. **Surprise subscriptions** (Humane, Plaud) → written "no subscription" promise + a plain "what you need" box (Piblo, Windows PC, Wi-Fi).
4. **Lost recordings, flaky sync** (Plaud) → voice notes saved on the device first, "saved to PC" tick. (Engineering task, not yet shipped.)
5. **Poor battery, unclear charging** (Cardputer: ~6-8 h) → measure and publish real battery hours.
6. **Laggy AI** (Friend 7-10 s, Rabbit) → show "listening / thinking" instantly; publish measured response time.
7. **Always-listening privacy fear** (Bee, Friend) → press-to-talk only, recording light on screen.
8. **Bad updates bricking devices** (Plaud, Cardputer wrong firmware) → signed updates with one-key rollback.
9. **Painful setup** (Cardputer drivers) → web flasher + one-click Windows Core installer.
10. **No support** → UK contact, stated reply times, replacement promise.
