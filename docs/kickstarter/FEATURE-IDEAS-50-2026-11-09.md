# Piblo: 50 feature ideas (v2, 9 Nov 2026)

**Filter:** a feature only makes the list if a phone, smartwatch or smart speaker does it badly. Piblo wins when at least one of these is true:
- **A. Tied to your computer and AI agents** (Piblo Core sees your PC, files and agents; a watch doesn't).
- **B. Glance without the rabbit hole** (no apps, feeds or notifications from anyone else).
- **C. Real keys** (type and press without looking or unlocking).
- **D. Visible to the people around you** (it sits on the desk, a phone sits face-down).
- **E. Safe to hand to someone** (a child, a parent, a colleague) instead of a smartphone.

Dropped from v1: breathing coach, water/stand-up nudges, mood check-in, wind-down, wake-up chime, kitchen timers, TV remote, Home Assistant buttons, language practice, Pomodoro. Watches, phones and remotes already do these well.

Effort: **S** = days, **M** = 1-3 weeks, **L** = month+. ⭐ = top pick for the campaign.

## 1. Your computer, in your pocket (A)
1. ⭐ **"What was I doing?"** (M): before a break or interruption, press a key; Core saves your open windows, tabs and last notes. Press again to restore and hear "you were halfway through the Acme invoice".
2. ⭐ **Find any file by voice** (M): "find the invoice from Acme in October" searches your own PC, shows the top 3, one key opens or sends it.
3. **PC errands** (M): "print my boarding pass", "send my last screenshot to Sam", "zip the project folder", each confirmed with a key.
4. **Long-job watcher** (S): render, download, backup, build or print finished? Piblo tells you, so you can leave the desk.
5. **Speak-to-type into any app** (M): hold the voice key, talk, the text appears where your cursor is on the PC. Dictation for apps that don't have it.

## 2. AI agents that need a human (A, C)
6. ⭐ **Approve any agent** (S, partly built): Claude Code, Codex, n8n, Zapier, GitHub Actions; yes/no from the keys, with the exact action shown.
7. ⭐ **Spending and sending guard** (M): any action that spends money, emails a customer or deletes data needs a long-press, with the amount or recipient on screen.
8. **Agent status board** (S): "3 running, 1 waiting on you, 1 failed"; press to see why.
9. **Brief an agent from anywhere** (M): type or say a task on Piblo, it is queued to your agent on the PC; result comes back as a short summary.
10. **Undo window** (M): for 10 minutes after an agent acts, one key rolls it back where the tool supports it.

## 3. Focus without your phone (B, D)
11. ⭐ **Do-not-disturb sign** (S): Piblo faces the room and shows "In focus until 3:00" from your calendar or focus timer. Family and coworkers can see it; a face-down phone can't do that.
12. ⭐ **Phone-free mode** (M): put your phone in a drawer; Piblo shows only the calls and messages from people on your VIP list (via Core), nothing else.
13. **Distraction parking** (S): one key, say the stray thought, it is saved for later without opening any app.
14. **"Just the next step"** (S): say a big task, Piblo shows only step 1, then step 2 when you press done.
15. **Body-double Buddy** (M): Buddy "works" on screen beside you and checks in by voice every 25 min, tied to what you said you'd do.

## 4. Never drop a ball (A, B)
16. ⭐ **"Waiting on" tracker** (S): "waiting on Sam for the quote" → Piblo asks in 3 days "did Sam reply?" and can draft the nudge email for approval.
17. **Smart snooze** (S): "later" means after your current meeting ends (from your calendar), not 10 minutes.
18. **"Where did I put it?"** (S): "passport in the blue folder"; ask later.
19. **Promise catcher** (M): in meetings, press a key when you promise something; it becomes a task with the person's name.
20. **Life admin from email** (M): Core reads your inbox for renewals, bills and appointments and asks yes/no/later on Piblo, so you don't open the inbox.

## 5. Capture and memory, private by default (C, A)
21. **Meeting marks** (M): record locally, press a key at important moments; summary and actions built around those marks.
22. **Private typed journal** (S): a real-key journal that saves to your PC only, never a cloud app.
23. **Voice notes into your tools** (M): "to Notion", "to Obsidian", "to Todoist", sorted by the AI on the PC.
24. **Offline capture** (S): notes save to microSD with no Wi-Fi, sync later with a "saved to PC" tick.
25. **Decision log** (S): "decided: we go with supplier B because of price"; searchable later, handy for teams and founders.

## 6. For the family (E, D)
26. ⭐ **A first device for kids** (M): reminders, a chore list with XP, messages only to parents, no browser and no apps. A step before a phone.
27. ⭐ **Parent check-in** (M): an elderly relative presses one big key: "I'm OK today" or "call me". Family sees it on their own Piblo or by email. No smartphone skills needed.
28. **House message board** (S): leave a typed or voice note for whoever comes home ("dinner in the oven").
29. **Shared shopping list** (S): anyone adds by voice; it shows on every Piblo in the house and on the PC.
30. **Chore rota with fair-share scores** (S): who did what this week, Buddy celebrates the winner.

## 7. Work and small business (A, D)
31. ⭐ **Lead and missed-call alerts with reply** (M): from a website form, receptionist bot or CRM; reply "call back at 3" by voice and Core sends it after you approve.
32. **Payment pings** (S): "£79 received from Jo" from Stripe/PayPal; one key to send a thank-you.
33. **Shift handover notes** (S): staff leave a voice note for the next shift; the next person presses to hear it.
34. **Counter approval box** (M): staff request a refund or discount on a till PC; the owner approves on Piblo from the back room.
35. **End-of-day timesheet** (S): Core already knows which apps and files you used; Piblo asks "Acme 3h, admin 1h, right?" and you confirm.

## 8. Developers and makers (A, C)
36. **CI and deploy board** (S): build red/green, one key to rerun, long-press to approve a production deploy.
37. **Pocket terminal** (M): run saved commands or SSH snippets on your own machines from the keyboard.
38. **Server and site watch** (S): "piblo.co.uk is down" with the error, one key to open logs on the PC.
39. **Pair-programming buddy** (M): ask about the code that's open on your PC, answer on screen, no tab-switching.
40. **Hardware hacking hooks** (M): Grove port and IR/USB for makers to build their own add-ons, documented.

## 9. Learning and thinking (B, C)
41. **Revision cards from your own notes** (M): Core turns your lecture or meeting notes into cards; keys for "got it / again".
42. **Hints-only homework helper** (M): explains without giving answers; parents set the rules.
43. **Socratic sparring** (S): "argue against my plan" for 5 minutes, typed, no feed to fall into.
44. **Reading capture** (S): type or say a quote and page while reading a paper book; it lands in your notes.
45. **Ask without the rabbit hole** (S): answers fit the screen, no links or feeds, so you get the fact and stop.

## 10. Trust, fun and sharing
46. ⭐ **Buddy that grows** (M): Buddy changes with your streaks; outfits unlock by finishing real tasks.
47. ⭐ **Privacy light and local-only switch** (S): visible recording light; one switch keeps everything on your PC with local models.
48. **Accountability pair** (M): link with a friend's Piblo; you each see the other's streak and can send a nudge.
49. **Accessible mode** (S): big text, spoken menus and high contrast for low vision, dyslexia or older users.
50. **Plugin store on GitHub** (L): community add-ons installed from Core, like the Flipper app catalogue.

## Campaign top 10 (one story per audience)
- Busy people: 1 ("What was I doing?"), 16 (waiting-on tracker)
- Focus: 11 (do-not-disturb sign), 12 (phone-free mode)
- Developers and AI users: 6 (approve any agent), 7 (spending guard)
- Families: 26 (kids' first device), 27 (parent check-in)
- Small business: 31 (lead alerts with reply)
- Trust: 47 (privacy light, local-only)
