## Biggest uncertainties
1. No bulk price for the Cardputer ADV or StampS3A exists in public pages. Every electronics number above is 1-unit retail. Distributor tiers are the largest gap.
2. Enclosure: no instant-quote figure was obtained for a 90x60x20 mm 6061 part with anodise and engraving. Only vendor-blog anodise ranges (REPORTED) exist.
3. Packaging: the only unit price is a supplier blog's "from about US$0.50" for a wine box, not a Piblo-sized rigid box. No UK 250/500/1000 price was found.
4. Shipping: Royal Mail Tracked 24/48 rates for a ~250 g parcel were not extracted (rate PDFs not parsed). Only aggregator examples for a 2 kg parcel exist.
5. Lithium: whether Royal Mail (or another carrier) accepts a Li-ion battery contained in equipment, and the packing rules, is not confirmed by a primary source.
6. Compliance: RED/EMC test cost, UN38.3 documents from M5Stack, and product liability premiums: none found.
7. Kode Dot figures conflict: Kickstarter page (16,193 backers, 3.37M) vs aggregator snapshot (8,646 backers, 1.75M, dated 27 Jul 2026). Use the live Kickstarter page with a caveat.
8. Playdate price: store shows 229 USD (out of stock) vs 149 in older reports. Use the store value.
9. Competitor prices are mostly LIST-PRICE from store pages, but Flipper Zero and Pebble are dated or launch-era REPORTED figures.
