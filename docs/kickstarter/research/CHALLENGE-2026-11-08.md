Tags: **[S]** = source URL checked today, **[E]** = my estimate (no published figure; get a quote). Not legal or tax advice: accountant and a compliance lab must confirm.

Headline: the docs' own central case at 500 units is roughly break-even (Original -£1.1k, Founder's +£2.2k, `EDITIONS.md:59-66`). ### B3. Being the "manufacturer" of a rebadged device (UK and EU)

M5Stack's FCC grant and CE test reports (EN 301 489, EN 300 328 BLE, EN 62479 etc.; https://fccid.io/2AN3WM5CARDPUTERADV ; https://openelab.io/products/m5stack-cardputer-adv-esp32-s3a) cover M5Stack's own product in M5Stack's own shell. A **metal** enclosure changes antenna performance and emissions, so reuse is not safe without retest.
