# Piblo Kickstarter handover, 8 Nov 2026

For the next agent. Read this first, then the files listed at the bottom.

## Goal
Maximise **real cash profit** from a UK Kickstarter for Piblo, a pocket companion on the M5Stack Cardputer ADV. Not revenue, not gross margin. Piblo must stay attractive to backers.

## Hard constraints
- **Maz's own cash: £300 maximum, total.** Ads, large tooling, compliance and stock must come from pledges, after funding.
- Nothing merged to main, and no prices published on the site, without Maz's explicit approval.
- Website prices stay unpublished until Maz approves. Free-model and paid OpenRouter use is approved within a **$1 total cap** unless Maz raises it. Never print API keys.
- Paid OpenRouter models are approved (Maz, 8 Nov). Free models (`opencode/deepseek-v4.1-flash-free`, `openrouter/free`) are fine for useful work.

## Key numbers (estimates, not quotes)
- Cardputer ADV: **$29.90** retail, verified on the M5Stack page (SKU K132-ADV). The earlier $79.90 figure was wrong.
- Original £103 / Founder's £194 (Maz's pricing baseline). Model: 80/20 mix, expected demand, VAT-registered.
- At £103/£194: profit before tax is **-£12k at 250 backers, -£19k at 500, -£3k at 1,000, +£148k at 5,000**.
- Break-even: about 1,005 backers at £103/£194; about 565 at £129/£249.
- Funding goal in the model: **about £49.5k**. Cash needed before payout: about £25k at 500 backers, £36k at 1,000. Both are far above £300.
- Biggest unknowns: price elasticity; Founder's CNC cost (no quote; the low-confidence research figure looks wrong); compliance (EMC/RED, UN38.3, PSTI, WEEE); M5Stack bulk pricing; international lithium shipping.
- Kickstarter fees: 5% + 3% + £0.30 per pledge (UK, list price).

## Decisions made
- The £300 own-money cap rules out pre-launch ads, the £7k video budget, tooling and a first batch before funding.
- Realistic route: free waitlist building; 2 Cardputers (~£54) for a real prototype clip; quotes first; launch only with a strong waitlist and a known compliance cost; a small funding goal that covers the first batch and compliance, not £49.5k.
- Pricing decision still open. The model shows higher prices (£129/£249) earn more at volume but carry demand risk.

## Known problems to fix before trusting numbers
1. The Excel workbook is not recalculated. Open it in Excel and check it matches `piblo_model.py`.
2. The model's "viable" test fails for every structure at 500 or 1,000 backers. Treat the model as a cost map, not a recommendation.
3. Competitor prices from the free model are unreliable; the Haiku evidence file has the usable numbers.
4. Compliance is paid after funding in the model. Selling in the UK needs it first, so that timing is wrong.
