## A6. Type and motion rules (1080 x 1920)

- Safe zone: 250 px top, 340 px bottom, 90 px sides. The phone is about 0.36 of canvas width, so type must be big.
- Hero or end line: 110-140 px, 2 lines maximum. Single-line cards: 96 px. Never below 56 px for anything the viewer must read.
- Honesty label: 36 px minimum, once at the start and once at the end.
- At most 6 words a card and 3 cards in 18 s. A card stays up for 0.25 s a word plus 0.6 s, so 6 words need 2.1 s.
- Bricolage Grotesque 700 for lines, Manrope 500 for the label. Left-align over footage, placed in the image's empty space. Centre only on the end card.
- Easing: enter with `power3.out` or `expo.out`, 0.45 s. Exit by cutting with the picture, not fading. Put the camera move on the picture, not on the text.
- Anti-slides: full-bleed pictures, no repeating paper background, no per-scene opacity dissolves (hard cuts or a match cut), a camera move in every shot, a different text position each card, a sound event on every cut, one idea per scene.
- Render the device on a real-looking surface with perspective and a contact shadow. Show screens blurred or idle until the real device is filmed.

## Cross-critique of the DeepSeek audit

Checked against `top.png` and `side.png` (about 7.5 px per mm). Measurements are read off the pictures, so roughly right.

**Correct**
- Our key colours are inverted. Real keys are black pills on white printed legend strips, on a light-grey body.
- The screen is about 29% of the width and left of centre (left edge about 21 mm in), not centred.
- Two-tone body. Missing from our renders: two screws, a mic hole, the "CARDPUTER ADV" label tile, the "M5" emboss, USB-C, a speaker grille and the 3.5 mm jack.

**Wrong or doubtful**
- Key size: 5.8 × 7.5 mm is pitch. A black pill is about 4 × 3 mm and wider than tall. Legends sit on strips between rows, not on keycaps.
- Raised block: the whole top strip (screen row plus Stamp) is raised, across the full width. It is not a 25 mm block at the right end.
- Heights: light top about 14 mm, dark base about 5.5 mm, not 16 and 3.6.
- Screws: about 13 mm and 55 mm across, at the lower edge of the raised strip beside the screen. Not "(24, 6) and (62, 6)".
- Stamp "metal shield can": unverified.
- Case "2.4 mm walls, snap tabs, layer lines" contradict the 2 mm wall in `NOD-FILMS.md`. Nothing is printed, so layer lines imply a finish that doesn't exist. Grove and microSD aren't visible in either photo.

**Film craft**
- Adopt: key plus rim light, off-centre framing, varied shot lengths, cutting on motion. This mostly matches A6.
- Reject: headlines at 8-10% of frame height (about 190 px, too big), background blur and handheld drift on CSS renders, and a uniform "2-3 s per beat".

**Change to my Top 8**
Move the render rebuild from #8 to #3, as a gate. No device render goes into a film until it is overlaid on `top.png` and `side.png`: black pills on white strips, screen left of centre, raised top strip, dark base.
