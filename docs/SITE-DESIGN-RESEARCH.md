# Redesign research (Nov 2026)

Looked at kode.diy, teenage.engineering (EP-133 page), nothing.tech and an Apple product page, at phone width.

1. **Kode is long because every idea gets its own full screen.** About 25 phone screens: a carousel, three audience sections, a 22-row chip list, box contents, videos. We keep one screen per big idea and fold the rest into a tap-to-open list.
2. **Kode's hero is one object on one flat warm panel.** Huge rounded taupe stage, device centred, name, one line, one yellow button. Nothing else competes. Direction B borrows this.
3. **Teenage Engineering shoots the product front-on, flat, on near-white.** No tilt, no scene. It reads like a drawing of an instrument. Direction A borrows this.
4. **TE uses tiny uppercase monospace labels** for names, specs and captions, next to a plain grotesk. The labels do the "engineered" work, so the page itself can stay quiet.
5. **Colour is rationed to one accent.** TE: orange on grey and black. Kode: one yellow button. Nothing: one red dot. The accent sits on the product and the main button, almost nowhere else.
6. **The product carries the colour, not the page.** In all four, the page is neutral and the one coloured key or button on the device is what you remember. Our mic key is the tomato one.
7. **Depth comes from light, not outlines.** No strokes around cards anywhere. Soft gradients, a faint top highlight, a wide blurred shadow under the object.
8. **Nothing and Apple alternate light with exactly one or two black bands** to reset the eye mid-page. One dark band is enough on a short page.
9. **Type scale is two sizes, far apart.** A very large tight headline and small calm body text, with little in between. Apple and Kode centre it; TE keeps it left on a grid.
10. **Navigation is nearly empty.** Logo, at most four words, one button that stays in view. On phones: logo and the button only.
11. **The main action appears three times:** header, hero, end of page. Never mid-section pop-ups.
12. **Specs are a quiet table,** not cards: TE's bullet lists and Apple's compare rows are plain rows with hairlines.
13. **Captions are honest and small.** TE and Kode both label renders and old designs in a line of small print right under the image; we do the same with "Concept render".
14. **Motion is slight.** Sections ease in once; the product never bounces. Screens glow softly.

What we did not copy: Kode's mascot-led sections and long galleries, TE's full-bleed photography (we have no photos yet), Apple's scroll-driven video.
