# nod roadmap

nod is built in the open on top of **MAZ Pocket**, a prototype on the M5Stack
Cardputer ADV with a companion hub (**MAZ Core**) on Windows.

Only items marked **Verified** have been tested end to end. Everything else is in progress or planned.

## Verified
- Hub answers questions using free cloud AI, switching automatically between providers when one is down or rate-limited, with a local model as last resort. (Typical reply: under a second.)
- Device connects to the hub over Wi-Fi and stays in sync.
- Wireless firmware updates.

## In progress — live testing on the prototype
- Hold-to-talk voice questions with spoken answers.
- Voice capture: brain dump and voice notes.
- Agent status on the device.
- Confirmed PC actions (the AI proposes, you press Enter).
- Phone-approved, time-limited PC control.

## Next
1. Fast cloud speech-to-text.
2. Natural voice replies.
3. Approve AI agent actions from anywhere, with status light and buzz.
4. Live agent lights: idle, thinking, done, needs you, error.
5. Voice to task: notes, tasks and reminders.
6. Action keys with Work, Home and Focus layers.
7. Daily brief and "what next".
8. XP and streaks from real outcomes.
9. One-step setup.

## Later
- Phone companion, automation triggers (n8n, Make, Zapier), Mac and Linux hub, community action packs.
- Custom nod hardware with a precision-machined metal Founders edition.
