# NOD consumer direction — updated 6 November 2026

**Consumer positioning:** Nod is an easy one-touch physical shortcut to genuinely useful laptop, TV and AI tasks, designed for ordinary people. The differentiator is not AI jargon: it is fewer clicks, practical convenience, local-first privacy, and **no mandatory Nod subscription for supported local workflows**. Some optional cloud/inference or provider services may cost extra.

**Core use cases to build and physically verify**: supported-local-media SUBTITLES for a laptop connected to a TV (find legally usable SRT or locally generate and load it), click/drop/assign simple household PC ROUTINES, and SAVING voice thoughts or safely organising files. Be honest about latency and compatible players/rights, and don't claim Netflix/Disney+/YouTube/DRM compatibility without real authorised integration and testing.

**Preserve existing MAZ Pocket v0.8.x features** as accessible advanced functions: CALL MAZ voice with spoken response/model routing; CAPTURE voice notes/brain dump/Teach Demo; AGENTS status/PLAN/CREW/RETRO/project builds; CONTROL Wi-Fi/pairing/PC scopes/phone grants; MEMORY Results Inbox/Prompt Deck; FOCUS timers/tasks/reminders; MAZ Core local Ollama and cloud choices; web and safe M5Launcher updating. Keep advanced features protected rather than deleting them in the consumer simplification.

**Current physical route:** start with owner's existing M5Stack Cardputer ADV, MAZ Pocket code and MAZ Core; design and print a distinct Nod shell with owner's 3D printer. No custom PCB/manufacturing tooling yet. Validate a reproducible, usable consumer product and realistic Cardputer-based founder kit economics first; crowdfunding can enable future manufacturing only after hardware/supply/fulfilment and safety gates.

**Execution:** Claude has ~20% usage left and is a thin orchestrator. Use the free OpenRouter quota and inexpensive paid models with a **hard $10 total inference cap across both batches**. Hermes and Telegram orchestration/testing are out; use a local interactive terminal or private LAN testing flow with the owner's physical participation. Preserve privacy/security and document test evidence.

**Marketing and funding:** record authentic 10-second smartphone clips of functioning device workflows; use [latent-spaces/brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag) with verified real-footage support/FFmpeg to render a polished teaser, plus uncut proof; create consumer website, genuine opt-in waitlist and Maz Works-style organic LinkedIn drafts. Study [Kode Dot](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/kode/kode-dot-the-all-in-one-pocket-size-maker-device) as campaign reference, not sales forecast. Prepare a realistic Kickstarter draft with genuine prototype, UK requirements, supplier quotes, fee/fulfilment/returns and seven readiness gates. Do not publish/live-fund without owner approval.
