# NOD consumer direction — 6 November 2026

**Nod is for ordinary people, not just developers.** Make useful laptop/TV/AI routines as simple as pressing a physical button and configuring a few clear drag/drop screens. The value is saved time, less friction, a physical non-distracting control, accessible convenience, free/local software where applicable and **no mandatory Nod subscription for supported local features**. Do not imply every cloud feature or AI model is free.

## Initial product thesis
1. **SUBTITLES:** user has laptop connected to TV and supported local media without subtitles. Press Nod -> compatible existing subtitle matching/selection, or local SRT generation and player loading. This needs real end-to-end demonstration and latency measurement. No universal Netflix/Disney+/YouTube claims or DRM bypass.
2. **ROUTINES:** select ordinary actions in a clear click/drag/drop PC companion; assign to a physical Nod button. Preview and permission controls; no hidden scripting.
3. **SAVE THIS / TIDY:** capture a thought into local note/task, or safe file tidy with preview/undo. Pick demonstrably reliable consumer option.

The device uses M5Stack Cardputer ADV initially; computation happens on a paired user's Windows PC (MAZ Core). Reuse MAZ Pocket v0.8.0 implementation and M5Launcher safety patterns. Future custom electronics are conditional on verified demand and manufacture economics. Owner has an existing 3D printer: prototype a reversible designed shell around Cardputer before contemplating a full custom PCB.
