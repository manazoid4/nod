FOUNDER REVIEW · 10 OCT 2026

Not ready to
rush.

Before NOD goes on Kickstarter, the prototype needs to work reliably, make financial sense and be honest about what backers will receive.

7 launch checks still openRead the current post-mortem ↗
THE DECISION

Prototype first.
Pledges second.

There is a real software foundation in MAZ Pocket, but NOD's combined voice, capture and physical agent approval experience has not yet been independently proven. A November campaign is a planning target, not a commitment.

01 / LAUNCH CHECKS

What must be true.

These are concrete completion criteria, not a speculative percentage score.

01

Working prototype

Record real Cardputer footage of the three core NOD journeys, with the paired PC shown.

OPEN
02

Safe agent approvals

Test permission requests, expiry, disconnects, repeated prompts and no-response fallback. No silent approvals.

OPEN
03

Easy first-time setup

Have at least five people install and pair NOD without a developer doing it for them.

OPEN
04

Real supplier quotes

Choose the Cardputer-based kit or custom hardware and obtain material, assembly, minimum-order and lead-time quotes.

OPEN
05

Compliance and shipping

Confirm product safety obligations, packaging, countries served, duties, support and returns.

OPEN
06

Viable unit economics

Price manufacturing, fees, tax, defects, fulfilment and contingency using real cost evidence.

OPEN
07

Honest campaign and demand

Show uncut device demos, gather opt-in interest, and verify Kickstarter requirements before offering rewards.

OPEN
02 / SIX PERSPECTIVES

Questions worth asking.

The full six-lens analysis lives in GitHub; here are the main decisions.

PRODUCT

Is NOD focused enough?

Ship Talk, Capture and Approve before gamification or app packs.

ENGINEERING

Does the entire loop work?

Bridge device, hub and Claude permission hook. Test on physical hardware.

SECURITY

Is every approval deliberate?

Verify identity, expiry, binding to exact action and fail-closed behaviour.

USER EXPERIENCE

Could someone set it up alone?

Make 240 × 135 prompts readable and setup/recovery effortless.

HARDWARE & COST

Can we build and deliver it?

Get real quotes, safety reviews and a returns allowance.

CAMPAIGN

Can backers verify the story?

Real footage, clear limits, honest progress and delivery assumptions.
