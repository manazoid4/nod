RESEARCH NOTE / 09 OCT 2026

Before the
big launch.

A public, evidence-led checklist for the NOD prototype. This is a planning audit—not an independent device certification, hardware test, or campaign approval.

NOT READY FOR HARDWARE PLEDGESSix review perspectives · Seven launch gates · One narrow MVP
THE OPPORTUNITYA physical cockpit for your AI

Make talk, capture and deliberate agent approvals feel immediate without opening another screen.

THE BIG RISKPromise outrunning proof

NOD-specific code is mostly draft. MAZ Pocket provides the deeper prototype, but the integrated Kickstarter demo is still unverified.

THE DECISIONPrototype first; sell second

Use November as a target review gate. Launch funding only after the hardware, economics and delivery plan are evidenced.

MULTI-PERSPECTIVE AUDIT

Six ways to
challenge nod.

Each perspective has one finding and a practical action.

01 / PRODUCT

Too many promises

The repo describes voice, notes, routines, agent control and gamification. Lead with three practical workflows; postpone XP/streaks and an app ecosystem.

Action: Freeze an evidence-backed v1.
02 / ENGINEERING

Integration gap

NOD repo contains a non-standalone draft. MAZ Pocket has a working base but core NOD approval features remain in separate pending work.

Action: Integrate hook → hub → device and test on hardware.
03 / SECURITY

Approval must mean approval

A tiny screen cannot safely explain every command. Require exact action/target, scope and expiry, deny-by-default, anti-replay protections and a tested fallback.

Action: Threat-model sensitive actions and run adversarial tests.
04 / USER EXPERIENCE

Small screen, big clarity

The 240×135 LCD demands short prompts, clear key labels, offline states and a quick way to view longer details on the PC/phone.

Action: Test first-use setup with five outsiders.
05 / HARDWARE & COST

Custom enclosure is not a product line

Battery, radio, tooling, yield, returns, packaging, VAT, shipping and compliance must be costed before assigning backer prices.

Action: Quote two suppliers and stress-test contribution margin.
06 / CAMPAIGN

Audience and evidence missing

A compelling campaign needs real uncut hardware footage, hands-on tests, honest boundaries, demand signals and an achievable delivery schedule.

Action: Build proof and waitlist before choosing a funding date.

GO / NO-GO

Seven gates to
Kickstarter.

No campaign launch until all seven can be backed by evidence. Tracking is informational, not proof.

TOP FAILURE MODES

How nod avoids
a hardware hype trap.

CRITICAL

Mock-up mistaken for a finished product

Clearly mark UI concepts. Campaign demonstration must be actual working hardware and disclose missing integration.

CRITICAL

Approval of a different action than shown

Sign request IDs, bind action and expiry to approval, reject stale decisions, and allow recovery on the terminal.

HIGH

Remote connectivity and privacy

Use authenticated private routes, protect credentials, avoid public unauthenticated PC control and display network failures honestly.

HIGH

Price without true landed costs

Get real production and fulfilment quotes before setting tiers or asking for pledges.

HIGH

Scope creep into a general AI phone

Prove one remarkable three-step journey; don't ship a pile of mostly-finished features.
