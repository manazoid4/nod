Make the physical promise dependable. Before more features, verify the device and its human-control boundaries.

THE STANDARD

Before we say
it works.

01

Real media, not guessed links. Video analysis must access the actual audio and visual content with permission and traceable sources.

02

Real hardware, not a render. Video demos must show the Cardputer and PC performing the workflow in the physical world.

03

A person decides. AI can propose a model and prepare G-code. A printer never starts without a deliberate, informed owner decision.

04

Make the economics real. Supply, support, compliance and shipping must be backed by evidence before any Kickstarter reward is offered.
