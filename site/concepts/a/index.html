What Nod does

Four things. One press each.

1

Talk, don't type

Hold a key, say what you need, let go. Nod sends it to the AI on your computer and gives you the answer, out loud or on the little screen.

2

Save a thought

Got an idea in the shower, or mid-conversation? Say it. It is saved for later and tidied up when you are ready.

Today

One calm screen for what is next and what is waiting on you.

Your PC asks. You decide.

How it works

A small device. A proper brain next to it.

  1. 1

    Nod

    The pocket device. A Cardputer ADV running Nod firmware. You press, talk and approve here.

  2. 2

    Nod Core

    A Windows app on your PC. It does the heavy lifting over your own connection.

  3. 3

    Your AI

    The assistant and tools you already use. They answer, or ask for your okay.

You need a Windows PC and a setup step. Some AI services charge separately.
