[01] What Nod does

Four keys. Four jobs.

  1. 1

    Talk, don't type

    Hold a key, speak, release. Nod hands your words to the AI on your PC and gives the answer back.

    In progress
  2. 2

    Save a thought

    Say an idea out loud before it is gone. Sorted and tidied later, when you want.

    In progress
  3. 3

    Today

    What is next, what is waiting, on a single screen.

    Concept
  4. 4

    PC actions, with approval

    An AI agent asks. Nod shows you exactly what. Nothing runs until you say yes.

    To verify

[02] How it works

The handheld talks. Your PC does the work.

DEVICE

Nod

An M5Stack Cardputer ADV running Nod firmware. You press, talk and approve.

WINDOWS APP

Nod Core

Runs on your PC and does the heavy lifting over your own connection.

YOUR TOOLS

Your AI

The assistant and tools you already use. They answer, or ask your permission.

Needs a Windows PC and a setup step. Some AI services charge separately.
