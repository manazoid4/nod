PIBLO / PRIVACY

Straightforward by design.

This is a prototype-information website with one data form: the waitlist. We do not run user accounts, advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts here. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views and button clicks without cookies and without following you across sites.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist we collect your email address and the name you type into the form. We only add you to the list after you tick the consent box, and we do not ask for anything else. Your details are stored with Resend, our email service provider, so we can send you Piblo updates. Each signup also sends a short notification email to the MazWorks team containing your name (if given) and email address, so we know someone has joined. Signing up twice does not create a second entry. After you join we give you a personal share link; we store its short code with your entry, and if you arrived through someone else's link we also store that person's code, so we can see which links bring friends. Your browser may remember that code locally (localStorage) until you sign up.

Why we can do this

Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box before you submit. We will only email you about Piblo: for example a working demo, a Kickstarter date or a price worth sharing. We do not sell your details or use them for anything else.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Update emails include an unsubscribe link. You can also reply to any email, or write to info@piblo.co.uk, to stop emails, to ask for a copy of what we hold, or to ask us to delete it. We keep waitlist details until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Contacting us by email

The Contact link opens your own email app. Sending a message contacts MazWorks directly and we use it to reply. Emailing us does not subscribe you to updates.

Website operations

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may process technical request data (such as IP address) as necessary to deliver and protect the service. Resend and Vercel are US-based providers, so your data may be processed outside the UK. Links to GitHub and other websites take you to services with their own privacy notices.

Future Piblo features

The proposed saved-video vault and model finder are not operational on this website. If we launch accounts or uploaded videos, we will publish the relevant data handling, retention and consent details before asking for that data.
