NOD / PRIVACY

Straightforward by design.

This is a static prototype-information website. We do not currently run an NOD account system, embedded analytics tracker or mailing-list signup form here.

Contact and email updates

The “Ask for launch updates” link opens your own email app with a pre-addressed message. Sending it contacts MazWorks directly. Your message and email address are used to respond and, if requested, communicate about NOD updates. Emailing us is not an instant newsletter subscription. You can ask us to stop contacting you or request deletion of information we hold by writing to info@mazworks.uk.

Website operations

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may process technical request data as necessary to deliver and protect the service. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts in this static site. Links to GitHub and other websites take you to services with their own privacy notices.

Future NOD features

The proposed saved-video vault and model finder are not operational on this website. If we launch accounts, uploaded videos or a proper mailing list, we will publish the relevant data handling, retention and consent details before asking for that data.

Notice reviewed 10 October 2026. Contact: info@mazworks.uk.
