How Piblo works

  1. 1

    Say it

    "Hey Piblo, remind me to call Mum after work." Talk naturally, or press the mic key. Cut in any time.

  2. 2

    It handles it

    Piblo remembers, sets things up and puts little helpers to work. Anything that matters waits for one press of a real key.

  3. 3

    It speaks up

    "Your train's delayed. Leave ten minutes early." Only when it matters, then it goes quiet again.

Straight answers

What works today?

On our prototype we have tested pairing with a Windows PC over Wi-Fi, showing its screen on the PC, and saving a spoken note to the PC. Voice questions work but answers are still slow. Reminders, alerts, the answer keys, approvals and Buddy are written and being tested now. Every feature on this page carries an honest label.

Who is it for?

Anyone who wants fewer distractions, starting with busy people and people with ADHD. Makers, developers and families get more on top: open code, add-on modules, and a real key to approve or refuse what your AI assistants want to do.

What's inside?

Piblo runs on proven open hardware inside our own case, so we can put everything into what makes it special: the conversation, the memory, the helpers and Buddy. Ask us anything on Discord.

Does it need a computer?

Not necessarily. Piblo pairs with the free Piblo app on your phone or computer. A phone covers everyday reminders and notes; a computer gives you the best voice. Android and Windows come first, with native Linux, macOS and iPhone support planned.

Does it work offline?

It needs your home or office Wi-Fi to reach your phone or computer. Reminders and notes are designed to stay on your own devices, without the internet. Spoken answers need a speech or AI service, which is usually online unless you run one yourself.

Can an AI do something without asking me?

Reading, summarising and reminding just happen. Anything that sends, books or posts waits for you: Piblo shows who, what and how much on its own screen, and only a press on Piblo says yes. Payments and deletions stay off unless you switch them on.

How is it different from my phone?

It does a few things and nothing else. No apps, no feeds, no notifications you didn't ask for. It shows one thing at a time and you answer with a real key, so you can deal with it and get back to your day.

How much will it cost?

Price to be announced. We'll share it with the launch list first. Shipping will be extra.

Is there a monthly fee?

No mandatory Piblo subscription. If you choose an optional outside service, that company may charge for it.

When does the Kickstarter start, and when would it ship?

There is no date yet. We'll only set one when the prototype is proven and the costs are checked, and backers will get an honest ship estimate based on real manufacturing progress.

Where is it made?

Designed in the UK. Some parts, such as the electronics, are made elsewhere.
