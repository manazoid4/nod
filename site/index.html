How it works

A small device. A proper brain next to it.

  1. 1

    Nod

    The pocket device. A Cardputer ADV running Nod firmware. You press, talk and approve here.

  2. 2

    Nod Core

    A Windows app on your PC. It does the heavy lifting over your own connection.

  3. 3

    Your AI

    The assistant and tools you already use. They answer, or ask for your okay.

You need a Windows PC and a setup step. Some AI services charge separately. Custom Nod hardware is not yet a finished product.

Proposed · not yet implemented

Saw it in a Reel? Make it real.

  1. Find the model. Look for the creator's real STL or 3MF.
  2. Match the right file. Compare it with what the video shows.
  3. Slice for your printer. Preview layers, fit, material and time.
  4. Your call. You check the printer and choose when to start.

Not a promise that every video contains a downloadable model. A person always starts the print.

Straight answers

Does Nod already analyse my saved Instagram Reels?

No. Video Vault is planned. We still need to prove importing accessible video, analysing both sound and visuals, and returning accurate source-backed answers. Instagram's saved-data export may provide links but not the video files.

Can Nod already slice and start a 3D print?

No. Reel-to-Print is a development concept. The proposed system would find a legitimate STL/3MF, verify its licence and suitability, slice on the connected computer and upload the prepared file to a print manager. A real person must inspect the printer and explicitly start the print.

What hardware and services would I need?

The current prototype uses an M5Stack Cardputer ADV with a companion Windows computer running Nod Core. AI services can have separate costs. The 3D-printing idea also needs your own compatible printer, a confirmed slicer profile and print management software.

Is there a Kickstarter date or price?

Not confirmed. A reliable physical prototype, safety testing, manufacturing quotes and realistic delivery costs must come before crowdfunding commitments. You can follow the source and ask for email updates below.
