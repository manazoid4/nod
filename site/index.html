How Piblo works

  1. 1

    Connect

    Join your Wi-Fi and pair Piblo with the free Piblo Core app on your phone.

  2. 2

    It tells you

    Reminders, meetings and alerts you chose arrive with a chime, one at a time.

  3. 3

    You answer

    Press a key for done, later, yes or no. Or hold a key and say a note.

What you need

  • A phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi running the free Piblo Core app. It stores your notes and sends the alerts. Android and iPhone come first; Windows and Mac apps follow. No computer needed.
  • Internet only for some things. Spoken answers use a speech or AI service you pick. Some are free, some charge, and you can run one yourself instead.

What it's for

  • Reminders

    "Bins out tonight." One press for done, one for later. Your phone stays in your pocket.

  • Meetings

    A nudge ten minutes before, so you're not watching the clock.

  • Voice notes

    Hold a key, say the thought, let go. It lands in your notes.

  • Alerts that matter

    Only the ones you choose. No app pings, no feed to fall into.

  • For work

    "Ticket waiting 2 hours" or "Build failed". Any tool that can send a webhook can reach Piblo, and you approve or reject with a key.

  • Find your AirPods

    Lost them? Piblo plays hotter, colder until you find them.

Yours to keep

  • No mandatory subscription

    Pay once. Piblo's own features won't sit behind a monthly fee.

  • Open-source software

    Anyone can read, change and fix it. See it on GitHub.

  • Updates and add-ons

    Software updates arrive over Wi-Fi from Piblo Core. The board has a standard port for add-ons; we'll only sell accessories once they're tested.

  • Not tied to us

    Piblo Core runs on your own device, not our servers. Outside services you choose may change or charge, so Piblo lets you switch.

Two editions are planned

Piblo Standard

The everyday companion. Better sound, with voice handled by your phone.

Piblo Pro

The more capable one. Talks back on the device itself, with better sound and a clearer mic. Plugs into your network and can see and control another computer.

Both editions run the same software and are expandable with add-on modules. Price to be announced.

Straight answers

What works today?

On our prototype (built on an off-the-shelf pocket computer) we have tested pairing with a Windows PC over Wi-Fi, showing its screen on the PC, and saving a spoken note to the PC. Voice questions work but answers are still slow. Reminders, alerts, the answer keys and Buddy are written and being tested now.

Does it need a computer?

No. Piblo pairs with the free Piblo Core app on your phone — Android and iPhone first, with Windows and Mac apps to follow. Your phone stores your notes and sends the alerts.

Does it work offline?

It needs your home or office Wi-Fi to reach your phone. Reminders and notes are designed to stay on your phone, without the internet. Spoken answers need a speech or AI service, which is usually online unless you run one yourself.

How is it different from my phone?

It does a few things and nothing else. No apps, no feeds, no notifications you didn't ask for. It shows one thing at a time and you answer with a real key, so you can deal with it and get back to your day.

How much will it cost?

Price to be announced. We'll share it with the launch list first. Shipping will be extra.

Is there a monthly fee?

No mandatory Piblo subscription. If you choose an optional outside service, that company may charge for it.

When does the Kickstarter start, and when would it ship?

There is no date yet. We'll only set one when the prototype is proven and the costs are checked, and backers will get an honest ship estimate based on real manufacturing progress.

Where is it made?

Designed in the UK. Some parts, such as the electronics, are made elsewhere.
