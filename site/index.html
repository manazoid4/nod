Three everyday moments Piblo is for

  • Bins out tonight

    A friendly alert on your desk or in your pocket. Press once for done, once for "remind me later". No phone to unlock, no feed to fall into.

  • Before the meeting

    Meetings, deadlines and business alerts arrive as one clear message. Approve, reject or snooze with a physical button, then carry on.

  • Say it, save it

    Hold a key, say the thought, let go. It is saved as a note you can find later, instead of living in your head or on a sticky note.

Also being built: a "task finished" ping when a long job is done, and simple automations you can connect to your own tools through webhooks. Today's early prototype shows voice notes reaching a Windows PC over Wi-Fi; the alerts and buttons above are in development.

Straight answers

When does the Kickstarter start?

There is no date yet. We will only set one when the prototype is proven and the costs are checked. People on the list hear first.

How much will it cost?

We are aiming for £79 for Piblo Original and £149 for the Founder's Edition. Those are targets until supplier quotes are confirmed, and the final price is shown on launch day. Shipping is extra.

When would it ship?

We are not promising a ship date. Backers get an honest estimate based on real manufacturing progress, and we will tell you early if anything slips.

What works today?

An early prototype built on an off-the-shelf pocket computer. It talks to a Windows PC over Wi-Fi and has saved voice notes. Smart alerts, the physical approve and snooze buttons and Piblo Buddy are still in development.

Is there a monthly fee?

No mandatory Piblo subscription. If you choose an optional outside service, that company may charge for it.

Where is it made?

Designed in the UK. Some parts, such as the electronics, are made elsewhere.
