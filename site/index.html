05 / FROM PIXELS TO PHYSICAL

Saw it in a Reel?
Make it real.

We're designing a path from a 3D-printing video to a real, printable model. The clever part isn't magically inventing the object. It's finding the original file—and checking the result before anything moves.

PROPOSED · NOT YET IMPLEMENTED
01 / DISCOVER↗

Find the model

Search the creator's links, captions and legitimate model listings for the actual STL or 3MF.

02 / VERIFY◇

Match the right file

Compare what the Reel shows with candidate models. Clearly label originals versus alternatives.

03 / PREPARE▤

Slice for your printer

Use a confirmed machine profile and preview the layers, fit, material and expected time.

04 / YOUR CALL✓

Approve the print

Queue a reviewed file in a connected print manager. You check the printer and choose when to start.

The aim: a real creator-linked model → a verified slice → a supervised physical print. Not a promise that every video contains a downloadable model.

06 / BUILDING WITH HONESTY

Ambitious ideas.
Clear limits.

We're exploring these experiences in the open. Here is what the prototype actually is—and what hasn't been built yet.

Read our Kickstarter readiness review ↗
Does NOD already analyse my saved Instagram Reels?

No. Video Vault is planned. We still need to prove importing accessible video, analysing both sound and visuals, and returning accurate source-backed answers. Instagram's saved-data export may provide links but not the video files.

Can NOD already slice and start a 3D print?

No. Reel-to-Print is a development concept. The proposed system would find a legitimate STL/3MF, verify its licence and suitability, slice on the connected computer and upload the prepared file to a print manager. A real person must inspect the printer and explicitly start the print.

What hardware and services would I need?

The current prototype uses an M5Stack Cardputer ADV with a companion computer running MAZ Core. AI services can have separate costs. The 3D-printing concept also requires your own compatible printer, a confirmed slicer profile and print management software. Custom NOD hardware is not yet a finished product.

Is there a Kickstarter date or price?

Not confirmed. A reliable physical prototype, safety testing, manufacturing quotes and realistic delivery costs must come before crowdfunding commitments. You can follow the source and ask for email updates below.
